# Supplement Product Overlap Review

Reviewed August 23, 2026. This is an editorial map for the site, not personalized medical advice. Product directions can change; the linked official pages remain the authority for current labels.

## Conclusions

- **Omega-3:** NORSAN Omega-3 capsules are the default fish-oil route. Sunday Natural EPA-focused and DHA-focused algae softgels are in the personal rotation. NORSAN liquid oil and Blueprint algae omega-3 remain non-default alternatives. The catalog does not interpret the rotation as taking every full label serving together.
- **D3 + K2:** Sunday Natural 2,500 IU + 100 mcg drops are the selected standalone route. The 20,000 IU tablets and altapharma drops are retired. Essential Capsules still contribute vitamin D, so the total amount remains visible as a caution.
- **Astaxanthin:** Sunday Natural BioAstin is the selected source. Blueprint Advanced Antioxidants is retained as a retired product rather than an active overlapping source.
- **Ashwagandha:** Blueprint Ashwagandha + Rhodiola and Sunday Natural Muscle Recover Ashwa Pro both contain ashwagandha, but at different amounts and with different companion ingredients. They overlap but are not exact substitutes.
- **Magnesium:** Magnesium Complex 11, Active Calm, magnesium bisglycinate, and Muscle Recover contribute magnesium in different forms. Tilman accepts this overlap; Active Calm and Muscle Recover are in the evening routine and extra magnesium after sport remains conditional.
- **Vitamin C:** Liposomal Vitamin C + Zinc and SunGlow Collagen + C both contribute vitamin C. Tilman accepts this overlap.
- **Essential Capsules:** The overlaps with the standalone B complex and CoQ10 product are accepted. Essential Capsules also supply vitamin D, which is why the selected D3/K2 drops retain a caution label.
- **NADH:** The B complex includes a smaller NADH amount and the standalone NADH 50 product adds another source. This accepted overlap remains visible.
- **Longevity Mix:** Retired. Its selected functions are distributed across standalone creatine, CaAKG, taurine, glucosamine, L-theanine, hyaluronic acid, vitamin C, and magnesium products. Glycine, lysine, and glutathione are not directly replaced by this order.
- **Collagen:** Sunday Natural SunGlow Luxe Collagen + C replaces Blueprint Collagen Peptides. The replacement differs in amount and composition and does not include the former type II cartilage ingredients.
- **Probiotics:** OMNi-BiOTiC SR-9 is the selected nine-strain probiotic product, with the personal one-versus-two-sachet schedule still open. Essential Capsules separately provide Lactobacillus acidophilus, so the two products are cumulative probiotic sources rather than exact substitutes.

## Label Conflicts Corrected

- Sunday Natural Vitamin D3 + K2 20,000 IU: current official directions say one tablet every 20 days with a meal, not weekly.
- Sunday Natural Astaxanthin 12 mg BioAstin: current official directions say one capsule every other day with a meal, not daily.
